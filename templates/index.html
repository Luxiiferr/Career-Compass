How CareerCompass Works

Three simple steps to discover your ideal IT career path.

Step 01
Share Your Portfolio

Submit a readable portfolio PDF, public portfolio website, or both, then complete your academic and interest information.

Step 02
Semantic Analysis

Sentence-BERT compares portfolio content with the defined IT career-track descriptions and combines relevant assessment inputs.

Step 03
Review Your Guidance

Explore your recommended career path, supporting signals, and alternative tracks for informed career planning.
